# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be welcoming, respectful, constructive, and evidence-based.
All contributors and maintainers are expected to participate professionally and to
focus disagreements on data, methodology, and documented reasoning.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Use inclusive language and protect other people's privacy.
- Support claims with traceable sources where practical.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
publishing private information without permission, and conduct that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `opensource@amadeus.com` while this
repository remains under Amadeus stewardship. Reports will be reviewed promptly
and confidentially.

**Before any transfer to the Green Software Foundation, this contact and the
enforcement responsibilities must be replaced or confirmed by the receiving GSF
project owners.**

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant. See its [FAQ](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/faq)
for guidance on interpretation.
